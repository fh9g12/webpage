      Gu, H., Healy, F., Rezgui, D., & Cooper, J. (2022). Sizing of High-Aspect-Ratio Wings with Folding Wingtips [Journal Article]. Journal of Aircraft, 1–15.
      
























      

      @article{RID153,
  author = {Gu, Huaiyuan and Healy, Fintan and Rezgui, Djamel and Cooper, Jonathan},
  title = {Sizing of High-Aspect-Ratio Wings with Folding Wingtips},
  journal = {Journal of Aircraft},
  pages = {1-15},
  issn = {0021-8669},
  doi = {10.2514/1.C036908},
  year = {2022},
  type = {Journal Article},
  file = {gu2022_folding_wings.pdf}

      This paper presents a comprehensive study on the sizing methodology for high-aspect-ratio wings incorporating folding wingtip devices. The research explores the structural and aerodynamic considerations necessary for optimizing wing design with these innovative features. Results demonstrate significant improvements in aircraft performance while maintaining structural integrity and operational safety.

      This experimental investigation examines the effects of folding wingtip devices on aircraft flight dynamics and pilot handling characteristics. Wind tunnel tests and flight simulations reveal the complex interactions between aerodynamic performance and control authority when implementing these morphing wing technologies.
